What we believe

Historic faith.
Everyday hope.

We hold to the historic Christian faith and seek to live it with conviction, humility, and love.

01The Bible

We believe the Scriptures are inspired by God, trustworthy, and our final authority for faith and life.

02God

We believe in one God—Father, Son, and Holy Spirit—eternal, holy, loving, and sovereign over all.

03Jesus Christ

We believe Jesus is fully God and fully man, lived without sin, died for our sins, rose bodily, and will return.

04Salvation

We believe salvation is God’s gift of grace, received through repentance and faith in Jesus Christ—not earned by our works.

05The Church

We believe the Church is the family of all who follow Jesus, called to worship, grow, serve, and make disciples together.
