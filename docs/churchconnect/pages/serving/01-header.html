Serve
Made to be given away
"For even the Son of Man came not to be served but to serve." Mark 10:45

Jesus washed feet, fed crowds, and gave His life. When we serve, we are not doing the church a favor. We are following Him. Each of us has been given a gift, and it was given to be used for others (1 Peter 4:10).

Serving helps the people you serve. It also changes you. It pulls you out of the back row and into the family, where you learn names and people learn yours. Most friendships that last in a church start on a team, not in a pew.

Pick a team below. No experience needed, just an hour and a willingness to say yes. Someone from that team will reach out and let you try it with no pressure.
