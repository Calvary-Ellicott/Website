Who we are

Calvary Ellicott

Our congregation is prayerfully preparing for a new season as a locally led church serving Ellicott and the surrounding plains. While our name and organizational relationship may be changing, our foundation is not: Jesus Christ, the authority of Scripture, and the call to make disciples.

We’re grateful for our history with Rocky Mountain Calvary and hopeful about what God is building here. We’ll share formal updates with clarity as decisions are finalized.
